Moving observer, moving stars

Biswajit Jana · Finding Earth 2.0 · 2026. The central question is how relative motion changes exoplanet observing opportunities. The model does not predict life or a count of future discoveries.

Galaxy: evidence and reconstruction

The measured layer contains 199 VLBI parallax entries from Reid et al. (2019), at catalogue epochs. Gold curves follow the published arm-fit intervals. The 190,000 background points are synthetic light samples, with an adopted bar and disk profile. Fainter arm extensions, luminosity weights and dust dimming are illustrative. These points are neither a full Gaia catalogue nor independently integrated stars. Measured overlays are hidden during future playback.

The Solar orbit uses a smooth static axisymmetric potential normalized to 233 km/s at 8.2 kpc. The outer warp is a separate kinematic illustration, with a 700 pc height scale at 14 kpc. It does not drive the orbit. Vertical display defaults to 1×; the bend tour selects labelled 5×. Optional light-pattern rotation is schematic.

Gas and gravity are separate models

The gas lab solves 2D isothermal Euler flow with a first-order finite-volume method in a prescribed rotating potential. Its raised surface graphs density. It is not a 3D Galactic gas reconstruction, MHD calculation or fitted future prediction. Stars and planets use gravitational trajectories, not fluid flow.

Solar System and reference frames

Approximate JPL inner-planet elements and saved Horizons outer-planet states supply the century scenes. The shared centre-of-mass reconstruction adds Solar reflex; the Moon is an illustrative orbit about the Earth–Moon barycentre. The original Earth & Sun scene retains a smooth Solar reference. In the 98% co-moving frame, the same translation is subtracted from all bodies, preserving instantaneous separations while making winding tracks easier to see. The ecliptic's roughly 60° tilt is relative to the Galactic plane, not a 60° inclination of the Sun's Galactic orbit.

A passive probe retains its release velocity. The cyan 2026 reference is a hypothetical fixed Galactic coordinate, not a naturally stationary spacecraft or an image of the past. Distance divided by light speed is an instantaneous geometric estimate, not retarded signal propagation.

Heliosphere and Space & Time

The 120 AU heliosphere is a scale illustration, not a radiation barrier or magnetic-fluid solution. Cosmic rays can penetrate. Space & Time shows either the moving ten-body weak field, an isolated-body clock comparison, or a spatial-slice diagram. The separate coupled 1PN lab includes a tested first post-Newtonian approximation; it is not full general relativity or a navigation ephemeris.

100 / 1,000 / 10,000 years

The report propagates 1,387 selected Gaia sources with constant relative space velocities. The longer intervals are nominal screening calculations without new uncertainty propagation. The isotropic direct-imaging score responds to distance and instrument thresholds; the calculated sky-angle change does not itself count new transits or discoveries.

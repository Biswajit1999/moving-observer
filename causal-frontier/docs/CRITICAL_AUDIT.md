# Critical Audit — Blocking Questions and Corrections

**Status:** Open review items; no new-physics discovery claimed.

1. **Background versus physical metric.** "Before ordinary light arrives" measures advantage relative to a reference geometry. It is not equivalent to a spacelike separation under a candidate curved metric. Keep two separately named metrics and separate outputs.
2. **Causal inference versus mutual information.** Shared randomness and postselection can create nonzero I(X;Y) without a causal signal. Randomized intervention and causal-side-channel audit are mandatory.
3. **Five sigma ambiguity.** One-sided and two-sided Gaussian tail probabilities differ; preregister the tail convention, number of trials, sequential stopping rule and multiplicity. No finite p-value alone proves FTL.
4. **Energy density normalization.** With u.u=-c², epsilon=T(u,u)/c². In c=1 unit-normalized tetrads, epsilon=T(e0,e0). Never silently mix them.
5. **NEC/WEC universal quantifiers.** Sampling observers can find violations, not prove universal satisfaction. Supplement with analytic algebraic classifications or certified global optimization where possible.
6. **GR inverse design.** A specified metric implies a required tensor, but does not prove any physical matter configuration can produce it. Enforce field equations, covariant conservation, boundary/initial constraints, regularity, dynamical stability and realistic preparation.
7. **Tensor mismatch.** Componentwise squared errors depend on coordinates and units. Use physically motivated orthonormal frames, invariant scalars where meaningful, and a dimensionally normalized loss; document weighting.
8. **Quantum energy inequalities.** Only apply proven bounds in their stated sampling, state, field, curvature and boundary domains. Avoid extrapolation to arbitrary dynamical Casimir or Planckian configurations.
9. **Planck cutoff.** Radius alone is not a complete validity criterion; track curvature and local proper gradient scales. Classical extrapolation near quantum-gravity regimes remains unreliable.
10. **Endpoint simultaneity.** The straight spatial corridor x_c(s,t) uses a chosen time slicing and is not a coordinate-invariant physical object. Test alternate slicings and formulate an invariant worldtube description if possible.
11. **Hyperwave provenance.** The research report cites a preprint, not a validated laboratory device. Verify latest version, full equations, units and all claims independently; never interpret small integrated energy as feasibility.
12. **Proxima dynamics.** Historical constant-velocity estimates and the cited 2026 encounter result are not interchangeable. Freeze epochs, model binary interactions and propagate covariance for long horizons.
13. **Optical budget.** Uniform beam-disc geometric collection is a rough order-of-magnitude model, not a diffraction-integrated Airy link. Peak power, receiver angular response, background and pointing can dominate performance.
14. **Quantum no-signalling.** Local marginals must be unconditional; revealing post-selected coincidences can require classical communication and must not contaminate pre-window data.
15. **Novelty.** "Microscopic warp communication" is pre-existing prior art. Proposed novelty must be limited to demonstrated new methodology/results and compared with citation networks and existing software.
16. **Computational proof limits.** No finite parameter sweep proves global hyperbolicity, absence of CTCs, or universal energy-condition satisfaction. Report sampled-domain limits.
17. **Experimental feasibility.** A desk simulation cannot measure real pre-lightcone propagation; physical hardware experiments can validate timing, noise and no-signalling controls, not a speculative warp geometry.
18. **Authorship and execution.** Initial files are AI-assisted research drafts. Tests are not represented as executed unless CI or a local runner confirms them.

**First implementation gate:** complete Lorentz/quantum/information controls and unit tests before speculative GR modules.

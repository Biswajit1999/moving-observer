## Relationship to this research branch

The `causal-frontier/` subtree on `research/causal-frontier-foundations` remains an archived early foundational staging prototype (19 tests passing when first checked). Its changes are intentionally not merged into Moving Observer's `main` branch.

The broader **Interstellar Link Lab** project extends this scope into three programme areas:

1. Photon Frontier — idealized optical link budgets, photon-counting PPM, analytical decoder error models, predictive image residuals, and CRC-gated per-tile receiver fallback.
2. Causal Frontier — Lorentz and quantum no-signalling controls, mutual-information checks and intervention-oriented causality tests.
3. Causal Corridor — validated flat/Schwarzschild geometric baselines, with speculative GR physics segregated until source, energy and causality checks pass.

## Scientific boundaries

- The current Earth–Proxima reference distance is an **illustrative static** 4.24 light years, not a frozen Gaia/Horizons ephemeris.
- Photon-link numbers are sensitive to extreme illustrative apertures and powers and are not a flight-qualified design.
- Real acquisition, forward-error correction, instrument validation, covariance propagation and speculative spacetime source validation are incomplete.
- Neither entanglement-only messaging nor an experimentally confirmed FTL corridor has been demonstrated.
